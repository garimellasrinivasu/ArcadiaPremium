The township

Thoughtfully planned

  • 430 plots across about 30 acres, planned as a gated plotted community
  • Mini township with an International School, Club House, Hospital and more
  • DTCP layout approval process completed (TLP No. 31/2026/H)
  • 40-ft and 33-ft wide internal roads, on the existing 100-ft road between Bidar and Pastapur
  • Three parks, plus about 600 acres of forest and agricultural research land next door
  • Plots from 150 to 471 square yards, all East or West facing

Why Zaheerabad, why now

A growing destination

🏭  NIMZ ZaheerabadOne of India's largest planned manufacturing zones (12,000+ acres), approved as an Industrial Smart City under the National Industrial Corridor programme.
🚜  Established industryMahindra & Mahindra's tractor plant and other manufacturers are already here, so jobs and housing demand keep growing.
🛣️  On NH-65The Hyderabad–Mumbai highway: about 1.5 hours from Hyderabad, close to the ORR and the Patancheru industrial belt.
🔄  Regional Ring RoadThe planned RRR in Sangareddy district will bring Zaheerabad closer to Hyderabad's growth.
🚆  Rail and airZaheerabad station is on the Vikarabad–Bidar line, and Bidar airport is about 40 km away.
🗺️  Three-state gatewayWhere Telangana, Karnataka and Maharashtra meet — a natural trade and logistics hub.
